# Polymarket Actuarial Model Governance

## Intended purpose
Identify point-in-time positive expected value Polymarket opportunities for research, backtesting and paper trading.

## Intended users
Quant, actuarial, model-risk and trading-system reviewers.

## Restrictions on use
Live trading is prohibited unless data readiness, validation, paper-trading evidence, risk controls and human approval all pass.

## Model risk
The model may be miscalibrated, overfit, affected by market microstructure, or invalidated by resolution ambiguity.

## Live trading approval requirements
Dual opt-in, approval file, no kill switch, no data quality blockers, validation approval, and paper trading evidence are mandatory.
