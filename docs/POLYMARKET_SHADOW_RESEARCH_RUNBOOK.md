# Shadow research runbook: retired local workflow

The Windows scheduled task, PowerShell launchers, direct Python loop and local
status checks formerly documented here are retired. Do not run them locally or
use them as an alternate scheduler.

Research/runtime recurrence belongs to the canonical VPS
`vps-ops-scheduler`; deployment and verification follow `AGENTS.md` and
`docs/POLYMARKET_VPS_DOCKER_RUNBOOK.md`. Current evidence is generated at
`outputs/performance/operating_state.{md,json}` on the VPS.

Historical scripts remain for regression/provenance only. Their presence does
not authorize execution, change an evidence class or open funding.
