# Venture Thesis — pre-registered, with pivot triggers

Filed 2026-07-11, before the Jul 20 policy date and the Aug 19-20 terminal
verdict, so the venture-level decision is as pre-committed as the trading
gates. Same discipline, one level up: no decision-under-influence, no
zombie grinding, no sunk-cost continuation.

## What has actually been built (the asset inventory)

1. **A candidate trading edge** (the thing being tested): maker carry +
   taker CLV on Polymarket. Honest current read: $0.93/day modelled maker
   carry at $500; taker lane underpowered at 8 units. May resolve NO.
2. **A research factory** (the thing that already works): an
   AI-orchestrated quant shop — orchestrator agent as quant lead, builder
   agent executing written work orders, human as operator — governed by
   pre-registered gates, tighten-only amendments, planted-truth tests,
   FDR discipline, telemetry, tamper-evident records (batch 5). Built by
   one person in under two weeks. **This is rarer than the edge.**
3. **A data asset that compounds daily**: full-ladder order-book history,
   reward-pool competition dynamics, print-level flow toxicity, resolved
   calibration corpus, wallet intelligence. Nobody sells this today.
4. **A provable track record substrate**: factsheet + hash chain + on-chain
   reconciliation, designed BEFORE results existed.

## The three-outcome tree (triggers bind at the registered dates)

**Outcome A — the edge clears (terminal verdict YES, or maker gates pass
and the live test survives its ladder).**
Path: proprietary trading. Scale via the WO-50 ladder and the WO-57
capital curve, never faster. The investor-grade record (batch 5) accrues
from live day one. External capital is a year-plus conversation (FSCA,
wrapper, custody) and only after 6+ months of reconciled live returns.

**Outcome B — the edge fails (terminal verdict NO on 2026-08-20).**
The venture does NOT die; it pivots to what already works:
- **Productize the factory and/or the data**: prediction-market analytics
  (reward-share modelling, toxicity feeds, calibration curves, maker
  yield screens) has no incumbent "Bloomberg". Candidate customers:
  active Polymarket makers/takers, the same people visible in our own
  wallet-intelligence ledgers.
- **The playbook as content/consulting**: "AI agents running a governed
  quant shop" is a story with an audience regardless of P&L sign.
Trigger discipline: if NO, no new trading lanes are built for 30 days;
the next 30 days go to customer discovery (below), then a written
decision: productize, shelve, or new-market restart with a fresh
registration.

**Outcome C — ambiguous (insufficient evidence even after the amendment-7
extension).** Amendment 7 already forbids this outcome: the Aug 19-20
read is terminal and resolves to NO on turnover grounds. Ambiguity is
pre-converted to Outcome B. No zombie state exists.

## Founder actions that beat any further code (all human, all cheap)

1. **Build in public, starting now.** The telemetry artifacts ARE the
   content: the factsheet, the decision-policy JSON, the repricing story
   ($59 -> $0.93/day when the honest share model landed) — that story
   builds more trust than a winning backtest ever could. One post/thread
   per week, generated from artifacts, zero marketing writing. Audience
   is optionality on every branch of the tree.
2. **Five customer conversations before Aug 19.** Find five active
   prediction-market traders; ask what they would pay for. If three say
   "the maker yield screen", Outcome B has a product on day one. This
   costs nothing and de-risks the biggest branch.
3. **The wallet dry-run** (standing item): Outcome A is only reachable if
   funding works. An evening of KYC/rails testing keeps the best branch
   alive.
4. **Bus-factor note**: the repo + charter + work orders are the playbook;
   keep them the single source of truth so the factory survives any one
   session, machine, or agent.

## What NOT to do (founder discipline, mirror of the gate discipline)

- No capital scaling ahead of the ladder. No external money before the
  wrapper. No second venue (Kalshi etc.) before the first one resolves.
- No SaaS building before five customer conversations say what to build.
- No new measurement lanes: the factory measures enough; the constraint
  is evidence-days and distribution, not code.

## Review cadence

This thesis is reviewed at the same checkpoints as the trading gates:
Jul 20 (policy date), Aug 19-20 (terminal verdict), and any kill-criteria
event. Changes follow the amendment discipline: dated, and only ever
adding precision or removing discretion.
